# Slide 2 - Current Sunco Line Review

## Role

Show where the new opportunity fits within the existing Sunco line.

This slide answers: "What do we already sell in this category, and where are the gaps?"

## Inputs

- Step 1 `Existing SKU Line Review` sheet
- Family Part Number
- Product Title
- Vendor
- Amazon Revenue
- Shopify Revenue
- Pack Sizes Available
- Optional fields when present: Vendor Cost, First PO Date, Active Status

## Layout

Use a dense line-review table on a white canvas:

- Top teal rule
- Centered title
- Main table below title
- Teal header row
- Alternating pale rows
- Source footnote above footer
- Sunco footer

## Sorting

Sort current SKUs by Total Revenue descending when available.

## Empty State

If no approved line-review rows exist, keep the slide but show a clear note:

`No approved Step 1 line-review snapshot was available for this category.`

Do not hide the slide, because leadership needs to know whether the line-review evidence is missing.

## Guardrails

- Do not pull old local line-review exports.
- Do not fabricate Shopify or Amazon revenue.
- Do not infer vendor cost unless the Step 1 line review provided it.
