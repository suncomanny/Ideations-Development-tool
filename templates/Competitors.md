## Competitive Analysis Framework

### Dimensions to Track (Category-Dependent):
- **Product Range** - Which form factors/types do they offer in each category?
- **Wattage Options** - Fixed vs. selectable wattage offerings
- **Color Temperature Range** - CCT options available
- **Features** - Dimmable, smart-capable, emergency-ready, energy-efficient, etc.
- **Pricing** - MSRP, distributor pricing, retail pricing
- **Availability** - Direct sales, distributor-only, Amazon/Shopify presence
- **Customer Reviews** - Rating patterns, common complaints, strengths
- **Market Position** - Premium, mid-market, budget positioning
- **Channel Strength** - Where are they strongest? (Retail, Distributor, Direct, Online)
